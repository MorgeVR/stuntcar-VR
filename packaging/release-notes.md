**Stunt Car Racer VR** - the Amiga classic in VR, for PC VR headsets and Meta Quest standalone.

### Downloads
- **StuntCarVR-Windows.zip** - PC (SteamVR, Meta Quest Link / Air Link, WMR) or a normal window without a headset. Unzip and read `READ ME FIRST.txt`.
- **StuntCarVR.apk** - Meta Quest 2 / 3 / 3S / Pro standalone. Sideload with Meta Quest Developer Hub or SideQuest (developer mode needed). Instructions in **StuntCarVR-Quest.zip**.

### Controls (VR)
Right trigger accelerate, left trigger brake / reverse, **hold both grips and turn your hands** to steer (or left stick), A boost, B turn around, X chase camera, Y recentre, Menu pause. PC also supports keyboard, gamepads and force feedback wheels (G27 etc.).

### Feedback
This is an early test build. Please report problems in [Issues](../../issues) with your headset model and the `log.txt` file (PC: `%APPDATA%\StuntCarVR\StuntCarVR\`, Quest: `Android/data/com.stuntcarvr/files/`).
