STUNT CAR RACER VR - Meta Quest (standalone)
============================================

One-time setup: enable developer mode
  1. Create / log in to a Meta developer account (free) at developers.meta.com/horizon
     and create an "organization" when asked.
  2. In the Meta Horizon app on your phone: Menu > Devices > your headset >
     Headset settings > Developer mode > On. Restart the headset.

Install
  * Meta Quest Developer Hub (PC/Mac, from Meta): connect the headset by USB, allow
    USB debugging in the headset, then drag StuntCarVR.apk onto the device.
  * or SideQuest: connect by USB and drag the APK onto the SideQuest window.
  * or adb:  adb install -r StuntCarVR.apk

Play
  In the headset: Library > (filter) Unknown Sources > Stunt Car Racer VR.

Controls: right trigger accelerate, left trigger brake/reverse, hold BOTH grips and turn
your hands to steer (or left stick), A boost, B turn around, X chase camera, Y recentre,
Menu = pause (B while paused = track menu). Menus: thumbstick + trigger.

Settings and log: /sdcard/Android/data/com.stuntcarvr/files/ (settings.ini, log.txt)
- the log is what to send if something goes wrong:
  adb pull /sdcard/Android/data/com.stuntcarvr/files/log.txt
